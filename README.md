# VYBE – Wear Your Vibe | BrandSell Live Marketing Project

**From a classroom marketing concept to a student-led business venture.**

VYBE – Wear Your Vibe is a live Marketing Management project developed as part of the **BrandSell: Brand the Unbranded** initiative at Lovely Professional University (LPU).

The project involved conceptualising a T-shirt brand, developing its identity, identifying target customers, implementing marketing strategies, managing customer outreach and tracking actual sales performance.

Rather than limiting ourselves to theoretical marketing concepts, we took a hands-on approach to understanding how a small brand operates in a competitive market.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Develop a distinct brand identity from scratch.
- Understand market segmentation, targeting and positioning (STP).
- Identify potential customers and understand their preferences.
- Implement promotional and customer acquisition strategies.
- Analyse competitors and product positioning.
- Monitor sales, expenses and overall business performance.

---

## 📢 Marketing Strategy & Execution

Our marketing activities focused on direct customer engagement and leveraging existing networks.

**Key marketing channels:**

- WhatsApp Marketing – Product promotion and customer communication.
- WhatsApp Status – Increasing product visibility.
- Personal Selling – Direct outreach to potential customers.
- Referrals & Word-of-Mouth – Encouraging customer recommendations.
- Instagram – Establishing a digital brand presence.

**Brand Development:**

- Created the VYBE brand name, logo and tagline.
- Developed a product range covering plain and printed T-shirts.
- Worked on brand positioning and customer segmentation.
- Conducted competitor benchmarking against established brands.
